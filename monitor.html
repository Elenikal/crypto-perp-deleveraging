State is a transparent score, not a model. A market scores one point for each of: funding beyond ±30%/yr, funding beyond ±75%/yr, open interest above 1.5× its own 24h volume, above 3×, an absolute mark-to-oracle premium over 20bp, and a venue leverage cap of 20× or more. calm is 0–1, elevated 2–3, stressed 4+.

The measure that matters most is OI ÷ 24h volume: it asks how large the standing positions are relative to the flow that would have to absorb them if they were force-closed. High funding says the crowd is leaning one way. High OI against thin volume says there is no one on the other side.
